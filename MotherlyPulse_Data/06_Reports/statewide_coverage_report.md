## 1. Executive Summary & Terminology Standards

This report provides the finalized, reconciled assessment of public maternal healthcare facilities and routine childhood immunization delivery points across all 18 LGAs of Ondo State.

### Terminology Baseline:
* **Identified in Registry:** The facility exists in the official National Health Facility Registry (NHFR) or state health records.
* **Partially Verified (51 facilities):** Established public health institutions corroborated by state directories, whose physical entrance gate coordinates, direct desk phones, or specific off-hour delivery readiness remain unconfirmed.
* **Verified Apex Institutions (4 facilities):** Major tertiary/specialist hospitals with independently confirmed physical campuses and Level-3 Comprehensive Emergency Obstetric and Newborn Care (CEmONC) capacity (`UNIMEDTH Akure`, `State Specialist Hospital Oke-Aro`, `UNIMEDTH Ondo`, and `FMC Owo`).
* **Confirmed Immunization Sites (4 centres):** Apex hospitals with daily fixed immunization clinics, continuous 24/7 birth doses, and dedicated certified cold rooms.
* **Provisional Immunization Sites (39 centres):** Public primary health posts designated for routine immunization, where **exact weekly clinic days and cold chain uptime are marked provisional**, advising users to confirm locally before traveling.

## 3. Map Direction Safety & User Guidance
* **Navigation Disclaimer:** The bot must never present community centroids as exact physical clinic gates. Where `coordinate_precision == 'community_centroid'`, the assistant informs the user:
  > *"This location is at the town centre. Please ask locally for the exact clinic building upon arrival."*
* **Immunization Session Disclaimer:** For provisional immunization delivery points, the assistant provides:
  > *"This facility is a designated primary immunization post. Standard clinic days are usually weekly or bi-weekly. Please confirm the exact session day with the clinic or community health worker before traveling."*
